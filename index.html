About Valley Electrical & Plumbing

Founded with a commitment to excellence, Valley Electrical & Plumbing has been serving Scranton, PA and the surrounding community for over 15 years. Our mission is to provide reliable, high-quality electrical and plumbing services with honesty and integrity.

Our Story

What started as a small family-owned operation has grown into a trusted name in the Valley region. Owner and founder, Michael Thompson, began his career as an apprentice electrician and quickly realized the need for a service company that combined technical expertise with old-fashioned customer service values.

Our Team

Our team consists of licensed electricians and plumbers who undergo continuous training to stay current with the latest technologies, safety standards, and building codes. We believe in treating every customer's home as if it were our own.

Our Values

  • Integrity: We're honest about what needs to be done and provide fair, transparent pricing.
  • Quality: We use only high-quality materials and stand behind our work with solid guarantees.
  • Reliability: When we say we'll be there, we show up on time and get the job done right.
  • Safety: We prioritize safety in every project, following all codes and best practices.
  • Community: We're proud to be part of the Scranton community and support local businesses.
